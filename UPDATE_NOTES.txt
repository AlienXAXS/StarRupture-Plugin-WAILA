- A server host can switch a kind of card off while the game runs, and WAILA stops showing it until the host turns it back on
- Card icons are drawn sharper, and the item icons on drone rail cards are larger so they are easier to read
- Card icons now come from the ModLoader's image library instead of WAILA's own icon cache. Cards keep their layout while an icon loads. This version needs ModLoader 1.24.0 or newer
- A storage or rail holding Ammo and blueprint items now lists each as its own item instead of adding them together
- Card icons now grow with the overlay font size, and the card widens to fit them
- A machine card shows the item a recipe makes with that item's own icon, so Sulfuric Acid is no longer drawn with the Battery's icon
